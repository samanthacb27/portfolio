My window faces a wall of trees, with a paper-thin opening that lets light filter through. Crowded around this window, the only source of ultraviolet rays, are my plants, each fighting for the space to stretch out their leaves and soak up the sun. Placed in random mugs, milk jugs, and containers I took from my mom, who is all too willing to give me her stuff, is an assortment of Golden-pothos. Each separate plant was once a trailing limb belonging to the original plant, Dave. The only plant I cared to name.
