This was my first time making a stop-motion animation. My set up included my nightstand, a bedroom lamp, and a tripod with my iphone to take each frime. I molded the catepillar using modeling clay and took approximately 310 photos for 13 seconds of moving footage at 24 frames per second. The project was exlusivley for fun and inspired many more stop-motion animation projects.
