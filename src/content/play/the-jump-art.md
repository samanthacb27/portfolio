I had taken a video of my friend jumping off a small cliff into the Ligurian sea. It was such an impactful moment for me, facing my fears in pursuit of experience. When it was my turn, I stood on those rocks staring down at the water for minutes, unable to take the leap. But I did, and it is, to this day, one of my favorite memories. 

This project is a reminder to always try new things, to experiment. So, with that in mind, I experimented heavily throughout the process. Inspired by Muybridge's *Horse In Motion*, I split the video into a grid of still images. I used a CMYK color separation and shot those separations onto screens with emulsion and a light box. Instead of using CMYK inks for my layers, I mixed my own ink, interested in seeing how different colors would effect CMYK separation. 

Once I had my favorite print, I decided to experiment once again, taking the still images and turning it back into a video via stop motion using PremierPro.
