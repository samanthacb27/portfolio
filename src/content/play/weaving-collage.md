In my sophomore year at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, I made this weaving as an exploration of my identity through the lens of heredity. Taking a concept of identity and looking at it in an extreme literal sense: I am half my mother and half my father. This project had me sit intentionally with images of my parents for prolonged periods, studying their faces and looking for myself in them. Through a quite literal construction of the theme, I made a piece that became quite abstract, and begs the viewers: are we our parents?
