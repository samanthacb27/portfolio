During my senior year at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, I made an animated documentary infographic about the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum theft using AfterEffects. This project taught me alot about how to keep the viewer entertained through constant movement, how to storyboard and how to connect written storytelling to visual cues.
