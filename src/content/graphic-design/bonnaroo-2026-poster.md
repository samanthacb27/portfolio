In my junior year at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, I took an advanced graphic design course under the guidance of Grayson Mendenhall, a graphic designer and animator based in Durham, N.C. For the course, I designed a lineup poster for the music festival Bonnaroo. Inspired by the festival’s setting on a farm in Manchester, Tennessee, and its established 1970s psychedelic aesthetic, I created a design featuring bright neon colors, hazy textures, and hand-illustrated elements. I wanted to incorporate Bonnaroo’s iconic arch while also creating a compelling narrative within the poster. With those goals in mind, I illustrated an alien playing the banjo, summoning a UFO to abduct the cows from the farm. This project taught me how to balance large, intricate graphics with the practical need to accommodate a significant amount of text, while still creating a cohesive and visually engaging composition.
