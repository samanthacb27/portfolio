In my junior year at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, I took an advanced graphic design class under the guidance of Grayson Mendenhall, a graphic designer and animator based in Durham, N.C. For the course, I redesigned the album cover and created a poster for The Colour and the Shape by the Foo Fighters, arguably one of the best albums by one of the best bands of all time. Inspired by the band’s name, “Foo Fighters,” a World War II term used by Allied pilots to describe unidentified flying objects, as well as the design style of Blue Note Records, I incorporated blue overhead lighting, UFO imagery, and a scope motif to create a retro-futuristic aesthetic.
