While studying abroad in Florence, Italy, I joined a student team at Florence University of the Arts’ Blending Magazine, working under Alessandro Calonego, FUA’s Head of Communications. I designed spreads for student-written articles in the published issue and submitted mock covers and section openers for consideration.
