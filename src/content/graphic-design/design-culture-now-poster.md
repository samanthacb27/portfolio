In my junior year at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, I took an advanced graphic design course under the guidance of Grayson Mendenhall, a graphic designer and animator based in Durham, N.C. For the course, I designed a poster for an event called Design Culture Now. The constraints: the typography had to serve as the central graphic of the poster. My idea was to expand upon a fundamental design principle: the Fibonacci spiral. Leaning into the fundamentals of design, I reduced my color palette to the primary colors. This assignment challenged me to create a poster that would be visually eye-catching from afar while also maintaining proper readability standards.
