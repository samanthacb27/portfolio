While studying abroad in Florence, I joined the student team behind designing *Blending*. The Fall 2025 issue was built around a single theme, *The Renaissance of Material Media*, and looks at the return to film, vinyl, and other physical media. I also wrote "The Art of Holding On" (pp. 50–51), an essay about junk journaling.
